Privacy Policy — Block Puzzle

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Block Puzzle (“the game”) is made by Wenja Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play. Contact: wenjagames@gmail.com.

Data the game keeps on your device

Your games in progress, scores and best scores, Daily Challenge results, achievements, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, we have no user accounts, and we do not receive this data. There is no sign-in and no analytics in the game.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is held by Google in your account under Google's terms; we cannot access it. Uninstalling the game deletes the data from the device.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (for example “Revive”, which lets you continue a game after watching an ad). Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one. The ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic and performance information.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive your personal data from Google; we see only aggregated reports about ad performance.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play; the game works fully without them and offline.

Deleting your data

Because game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
